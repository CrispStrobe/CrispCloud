CrispCloud Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 10, 2026

Summary

CrispCloud does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us. All your files, credentials, and settings stay on your device or go directly to the cloud providers you connect (Internxt, Filen, pCloud, SFTP, FTP, WebDAV). We have no servers that receive your data.

Data We Collect

None. CrispCloud is a local-first application. We do not operate any backend services, analytics endpoints, or telemetry servers.

Cloud Provider Credentials

When you connect a cloud storage account, your credentials are stored locally on your device using the platform's secure storage (Keychain on iOS/macOS, Android Keystore, or encrypted storage on desktop). Credentials are sent only to the respective cloud provider's API endpoints — never to us.

Optional Encryption

CrispCloud offers optional client-side AES-256-GCM encryption for files before upload. Encryption keys are derived locally and never leave your device.

Analytics

CrispCloud includes an opt-in analytics toggle in Settings. When disabled (the default), no usage data is collected or transmitted. When enabled, anonymous usage statistics may be collected locally for diagnostic purposes only — they are not sent to any external service.

Third-Party Services

CrispCloud connects to the cloud providers you explicitly configure. Each provider has its own privacy policy. CrispCloud itself does not share data with any advertising, analytics, or data-brokerage service.

Children's Privacy

CrispCloud does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app is rated for all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL. Major changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
